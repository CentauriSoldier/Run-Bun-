## Glossary

<!-- section: glossary-brace; source: 0 -->
### Brace

The pair of Buns belonging to one player.

<!-- section: glossary-bun-bunny; source: 0 -->
### Bun

Interchangeable terms for a character controlled by a Bun player.

<!-- section: glossary-burrow; source: 0 -->
### Burrow

A section on a Bun card. Each escaped Bun gives its player up to one play using that section per Dog turn.

<!-- section: glossary-burrowed; source: 0 -->
### Burrowed

A Bun in the Burrow area. An Injured Bun is Hidden and cannot play Burrow cards.

<!-- section: glossary-catch; source: 0 -->
### Catch

The Dog catches the chased Bun. Catch and Escape are mutually exclusive:
when a Chase resolves, either the Dog catches the Bun or the Bun escapes.

During a Chase, the Dog makes a catch roll and the Bun makes an escape roll using d20s. Apply the relevant modifiers; the higher total wins.
A Dog win means Catch; a Bun win means Escape.

Natural rolls take precedence over modified totals. A natural 20 succeeds and a natural 1 fails: a Dog natural 20 or Bun natural 1 means Catch; a Bun natural 20 or Dog natural 1 means Escape. If both roll natural 20s or both roll natural 1s, both reroll instead.

If neither rolls a natural 1 or 20 and their modified totals tie, both reroll. Repeat until the Chase resolves.

<!-- section: glossary-catch-roll-escape-roll; source: 0 -->
### Catch Roll

See Catch.

<!-- section: glossary-chase; source: 0 -->
### Chase

A Dog card that starts a pursuit. The pursuit itself is called a Chase.

<!-- section: glossary-chase-modifier; source: 0 -->
### Chase Modifier

The value printed on a Bun card that adjusts its escape roll in a Chase.

<!-- section: glossary-contend; source: 0 -->
### Contend

Any adversarial Bun-versus-Bun roll.

<!-- section: glossary-cozy; source: 0 -->
### Cozy

A Cozy Bun cannot be targeted by a Chase unless the Dog has used Prowl on **that Bun**. If Cozy is applied in response to a Chase that it prevents, cancel that Chase. Playing Prowl on a different Bun does not bypass Cozy.

Cozy's duration and whether an earlier turn's Prowl counts remain undecided.

<!-- section: glossary-cunning; source: 0 -->
### Cunning

A numeric stat used in Guard contests. Participating Buns combine their Cunning values against the Dog's Cunning value. The Dog trait also named Cunning is distinct from this stat.

<!-- section: glossary-dawn; source: 0 -->
### Dawn

The first interval of the game, followed by Noon. Play through a series of Dog turns until every Bun has escaped or been caught and either eaten or released.

<!-- section: glossary-day; source: 0 -->
### Day

The full game, consisting of three intervals played in order: Dawn, Noon, and Dusk.

<!-- section: glossary-divert; source: 0 -->
### Divert

An effect that sends a Chase toward a different Bun.

<!-- section: glossary-dog; source: 0 -->
### Dog

The character that pursues the Buns, or the player controlling it.

<!-- section: glossary-dusk-dawn; source: 0 -->
### Dusk

The third and final interval of the game, following Noon. Play through Dog turns until every Bun has escaped or been caught and either eaten or released. Completing Dusk ends the game.

<!-- section: glossary-eat; source: 0 -->
### Eat

Remove the caught Bun from the game permanently. The Dog gains 1 point, and the Bun's player loses 1 point. Applicable Brace bonuses and Natural Roll adjustments are separate.

<!-- section: glossary-escape; source: 0 -->
### Escape

The chased Bun escapes the Dog instead of being caught. Escape is the opposite outcome to Catch; only one can occur when a Chase resolves.

The Bun escapes by winning the opposed Chase roll. A Bun natural 20 or Dog natural 1 means Escape, except that matching natural 20s or matching natural 1s make both sides reroll. See Catch for the shared roll, modifier and tie rules.

<!-- section: glossary-escape-roll; source: 0 -->
### Escape Roll

See Escape.

<!-- section: glossary-expose; source: 0 -->
### Expose

Turn a Hidden Bun card face up. This applies to any Bun card, including Prep cards.

<!-- section: glossary-exposed; source: 0 -->
### Exposed

A Bun card that is face up. This term applies to any Bun card, including Prep cards.

<!-- section: glossary-guard; source: 0 -->
### Guard

A Survival card that commits one Roaming Bun you control to protect one chased Bun. The defense uses the participating Buns' Cunning against the Dog's Cunning. Success cancels the Chase; failure subjects all participating Buns, including helpers, to the Chase. See Card Types for the full Guard rules.

<!-- section: glossary-havoc; source: 0 -->
### Havoc

The Dog's action deck, containing Chase, Lure, Prowl, and Startle cards.

<!-- section: glossary-hidden; source: 0 -->
### Hidden

A Bun card that is face down. This term applies to any Bun card, including Prep cards.

<!-- section: glossary-hide; source: 0 -->
### Hide

Turn an Exposed Bun card face down. This applies to any Bun card, including Prep cards.

<!-- section: glossary-injured; source: 0 -->
### Injured

An Injured Bun is one the Dog has released after catching it. It is placed Hidden (face down) in the Burrow area and cannot play Burrow cards.

<!-- section: glossary-interval; source: 0 -->
### Interval

One of the three parts of a full game's day: Dawn, Noon, and Dusk, played in that order. Each interval contains a series of Dog turns and ends when every Bun has escaped or been caught and either eaten or released. The interval card tracks progress from its top section to its bottom section.
Each interval will have its own modifiers; those modifiers remain to be defined.

<!-- section: glossary-last-chase; source: 0 -->
### Last Chase

A final, sudden-death Chase roll that decides the winner when the Dog ties with a Bun for first place at the end of the game. The Dog makes a d20 catch roll and the Bun makes a d20 escape roll. All active modifiers that apply to those rolls for the Dog and Bun apply. Use the normal Catch and Escape rules, including natural rolls and rerolls.

<!-- section: glossary-lucky; source: 0 -->
### Lucky

Roll twice and take the better of the two results.

If Lucky specifies a type of roll, it applies only to that type. For example, **Lucky on escape rolls** applies only to escape rolls. If no type is specified, Lucky applies to all rolls.

<!-- section: glossary-lure; source: 0 -->
### Lure

A Dog card that baits a Bun or sets up a Chase.

<!-- section: glossary-nasty; source: 0 -->
### Nasty

Nasty cards make trouble for another player's Bun.

<!-- section: glossary-natural-roll; source: 0 -->
### Natural Roll

The number showing on the die before modifiers.

<!-- section: glossary-nice-nasty; source: 0 -->
### Nice

Nice cards help another player's Bun.

<!-- section: glossary-noon; source: 0 -->
### Noon

The second interval of the game, between Dawn and Dusk. Play through Dog turns until every Bun has escaped or been caught and either eaten or released.

<!-- section: glossary-panic; source: 0 -->
### Panic

A Reaction card only the chased Bun's owner may play for that Bun, in response to Startle.

<!-- section: glossary-prep; source: 0 -->
### Prep

A Bun card kept Hidden in its owner's area until activated. It is not assigned to a Bun in advance. Choose eligible targets when activating it, apply its effect, and discard it after resolution.

<!-- section: glossary-prowl; source: 0 -->
### Prowl

A Dog card that reveals Buns. It cannot catch them; catching requires a Chase.

<!-- section: glossary-reaction; source: 0 -->
### Reaction

A card played in response to an event. For example, Panic responds to Startle during a Chase against your Bun.

<!-- section: glossary-release; source: 0 -->
### Release

Applies only if the Dog catches a Bun. The Bun's player loses points, and the Dog gains no points. Place the released Bun in the Burrow area, Hidden and Injured.

The point loss is intended to be high; the exact amount remains undecided.

<!-- section: glossary-roaming; source: 0 -->
### Roaming

A Bun that is active in play and has not burrowed, whether face up or face down.

<!-- section: glossary-scavenge; source: 0 -->
### Scavenge

A Survival card for finding or exchanging supplies. Its effect requires a successful Scavenge Check using the acting Roaming Bun's Scavenge value and the card's modifier. It may involve your hand, the Survival deck, the shared Survival discard pile, or your own Hidden Prep, as specified by the card. Other players' hands are off-limits.

<!-- section: glossary-scavenge-check; source: 0 -->
### Scavenge Check

Roll a d20, add the acting Roaming Bun's Scavenge value and the Scavenge card's modifier. A total of 16 or higher succeeds. A natural 20 always succeeds and a natural 1 always fails, regardless of modifiers.
Each Roaming Bun provides one independent Scavenge opportunity per Dog turn during Preparation. Burrowed Buns do not contribute.

<!-- section: glossary-startle; source: 0 -->
### Startle

A Havoc card that allows the chased Bun's owner to respond with Panic for that Bun. Its individual effects are specified by the card.

<!-- section: glossary-survival; source: 0 -->
### Survival

The Buns' shared action deck, containing Nice, Nasty, Panic, Prep, Scavenge, and Guard cards.

<!-- section: glossary-traits; source: 0 -->
### Traits

Named attributes on Bun and Dog cards that card effects can refer to. Trait names have no inherent mechanical effect; the referring card specifies what happens.

<!-- section: glossary-unlucky; source: 0 -->
### Unlucky

Roll twice and take the worse of the two results.

If Unlucky specifies a type of roll, it applies only to that type. For example, **Unlucky on escape rolls** applies only to escape rolls. If no type is specified, Unlucky applies to all rolls.
