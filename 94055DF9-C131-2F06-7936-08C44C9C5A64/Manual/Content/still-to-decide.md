# 🔍 Still to decide

- **Other Dog play limits and interval endings.** One Chase per Dog turn and one Startle per Chase are agreed. Set limits for Prowl and Lure, when the Dog must use a Chase, and how play continues if the Dog draws no Chase cards or temporarily has no eligible targets.
- **Remaining Bun allowances.** The card-type windows are now in Take a Dog Turn. Reconcile the older regular/Burrow allowances with the new limits, settle remaining Burrow timing, and decide how an escape affects a play already spent that turn, and how to choose replacement Buns.
- **Card effects and Diverts.** Define individual cards, including how Lures and Diverts interact with Panic and the once-per-turn target limit. Check that hiding and Diverts cannot prevent the interval from ending indefinitely.

Earlier wording is retained in OLD subsections beside the matching rules.

# Open details from the current playtest

- **Terminology change deferred:** rename "Dog turn" to "round" in a later agreed pass. Current rules retain "Dog turn" until that update.

- **Prep effect order and duration:** ordering when multiple Prep effects apply, and whether protection worded "this turn" continues after its card is discarded, remain undecided.

- **Cozy:** duration and Prowl lookback.
- **Guard:** eligibility of already-chased helpers; how multiple Guard cards and defended groups combine; ties, rerolls, natural-roll scoring, and whether Panic modifiers carry into Guard.
- **Failed Guard:** determine whether the Dog rolls once or separately against the original target and exposed helpers, and the order of their rolls. This is separate from card-granted additional Chases, which resolve sequentially as brand-new Chases.
- **Canceled Chases:** destination of the Chase card and treatment of previously spent reactions and committed helpers.
- **Cunning:** assign and balance card values; distinguish the numeric stat from the existing Dog trait of the same name. No sample values are final.
- **Scavenge resolution:** decide the destination of a Scavenge card after a successful or failed check, and define individual effects and modifiers.
- **Scavenge and Guard production:** create the actual cards and implement their effects. Their rules are documented; automated enforcement is not implied.
- **Rule-changing cards:** retain the future idea of cards that alter rules; effects, duration, scope, and whether these form a separate type are open.
