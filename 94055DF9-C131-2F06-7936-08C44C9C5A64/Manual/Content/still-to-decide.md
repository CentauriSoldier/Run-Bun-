## 🔍 Still to decide

- **Other Dog play limits and Dusk.** One Chase per Dog turn and one Startle
  per Chase are agreed. Set limits for Sniff and Lure, when the Dog must
  use a Chase, and how play continues if the Dog draws no Chase cards or
  temporarily has no eligible targets.
- **Remaining Bun allowances.** The card-type windows are now in Take a Dog
  Turn. Reconcile the older regular/Burrow allowances with the new limits,
  set Scavenge limits and remaining Burrow timing, and decide how
  an escape affects a play already spent that turn, and how to choose
  replacement Buns.
- **Card effects and Redirects.** Define individual cards, including how Lures
  and Redirects interact with Panic and the once-per-turn target limit. Check
  that hiding and Redirects cannot prevent the day from ending indefinitely.

The original notes preserve the earlier version.

## Open details from the current playtest

- **Cozy:** duration, Sniff lookback, and partial protection in a multi-Bun Chase.
- **Guard:** number of Guard cards allowed in each player's opportunity;
  eligibility of already-chased or burrowed helpers; how multiple Guard cards
  and defended groups combine; who rolls the shared die; ties, rerolls,
  natural-roll scoring, and whether Panic modifiers carry into Guard.
- **Multiple targets:** which owner starts the Guard window if different
  players are targeted; whether the Dog rolls once or separately for the
  resulting Chases; Panic allowance when several Buns are targeted.
- **Canceled Chases:** destination of the Chase card and treatment of
  previously spent reactions and committed helpers.
- **Cunning:** assign and balance card values; distinguish the numeric stat
  from the existing Dog trait of the same name. No sample values are final.
- **Scavenge and Guard production:** create the actual cards and implement
  their effects. Their rules are documented; automated enforcement is not
  implied.
- **Rule-changing cards:** retain the future idea of cards that alter rules;
  effects, duration, scope, and whether these form a separate type are open.
