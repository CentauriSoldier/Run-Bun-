# Starting The Game

A full game of **Run Bun!** lasts one day, played through three **intervals**: **Dawn**, **Noon**, and **Dusk**.

# Name the day

Before the first interval begins, the Dog player draws a card from the Almanac deck and places it in the play area faceup.

## Follow the day

**Dawn → Noon → Dusk → End of Game**

1. **Dawn.** Begin the first interval and play until every Bun has escaped or been caught and either eaten or injured.
2. **Noon.** Move to the second interval and play until the Buns have again escaped or been caught and either eaten or injured.
3. **Dusk.** Play the final interval to the same conclusion. Completing Dusk ends the game.

Each interval changes play through its own modifiers, giving Dawn, Noon, and Dusk a different flavor.

## Track the interval

The **interval card** shows the game's progress through three illustrated sections: Dawn at the top, Noon in the middle, and Dusk at the bottom.
Follow these sections in order as the game advances through its day.
