# The Game

A full game of **Run Bun!** lasts one day, played through three
**intervals**: **Dawn**, **Day**, and **Dusk**.

## Name the day

Before the first interval begins, the Dog player names the day. It might
be the **Day of the Frost**, the **Day of the Monkey**, or the **Day of
the Reaper**. Declaring the name is a tradition that gives each game its
own character.

## Follow the day

**Name the day → Dawn → Day → Dusk → End of game**

1. **Dawn.** Begin the first interval and play until every Bun has escaped
   or been caught and either eaten or released.
2. **Day.** Move to the second interval and play until the Buns have again
   escaped or been caught and either eaten or released.
3. **Dusk.** Play the final interval to the same conclusion. Completing
   Dusk ends the game.

Each interval changes play through its own modifiers, giving Dawn, Day,
and Dusk a different character. The specific interval modifiers are still
to be defined.

## Track the interval

The **interval card** shows the game's progress through three illustrated
sections: Dawn at the top, Day in the middle, and Dusk at the bottom.
Follow these sections in order as the game advances through its day.
