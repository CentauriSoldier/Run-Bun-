# 🥕 The one-minute teach

**3–8 players · 1 Dog · 2 Buns per Bun player · 1 day · 3 intervals**

A fast, loud steampunk Chase. Help a rival get away when the Dog is ahead.
Send the Chase their way when they’re pulling ahead of you.

One player is the Dog. Everyone else starts with two Bun, called a Brace. Buns enter play face down; only their Brace’s owner may look at them. The Dog draws cards and plays Chase to pursue a Bun. Its player can react with Panic, then both sides roll a d20. If the Dog wins, it catches the Bun and chooses to eat or injure it. If the Bun wins, it escapes for the interval.

Every Bun you get to safety keeps you in the action from the Burrow.
Injured Buns sit out until the next interval. Once every Bun has escaped, been eaten, or been injured, the interval ends and everyone scores. Before the next interval, escaped and injured Buns return to their Braces, and fresh Buns take the places of any the Dog ate. Get the same Bun out again and its escape is worth even more.

After Dusk, add up the scores. The highest total wins. Whether you played the table to keep your Brace alive or timed your Chases to catch the Buns that mattered most, three intervals of sharp decisions earned you the win.
