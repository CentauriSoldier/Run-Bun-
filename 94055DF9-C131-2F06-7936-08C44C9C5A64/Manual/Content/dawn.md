# 🌅 Reset Between Intervals

After scoring Dawn or Noon, reset the Buns for the next interval and advance the Interval card. After Dusk, total the scores instead; the game is over.

- **Escaped:** the same Bun returns to play and keeps its escape history.
- **Injured:** the same Bun returns to play and keeps its escape history.
- **Eaten:** that Bun is gone for the rest of the game. Fill its slot with a new Bun with no escape history.

For example, if one Bun escaped and its partner was eaten during Dawn, you start Noon with the Bun that escaped and one new Bun. Your Dawn score stays recorded.
