## Setup

1. **Choose the Dog player.** The lowest-scoring player from the previous
   game plays the Dog. If this is the first game, everyone rolls a d20. If
   players tied for the lowest score, only those players roll. The lowest
   roll gets the Dog. Reroll among players tied for the lowest roll until
   one player gets it. Everyone else plays as a Bun player.
2. **Establish turn order.** Start with the Dog player and go clockwise
   around the table. Use this order for the starting hands and Bun draws.
3. **Shuffle and lay out the decks.** Shuffle the Dog and Bun character
   decks separately and keep them face down within reach. Shuffle Chase,
   Lure, Prowl, and Startle together to make the Dog's Havoc draw pile.
   Shuffle Nice, Nasty, Panic, and Prep together to make the Buns' Survival
   draw pile. Place both
   draw piles face down and leave space for a separate discard pile beside
   each.
