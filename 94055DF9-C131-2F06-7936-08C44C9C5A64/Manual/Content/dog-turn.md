## Take a **Dog** Turn 🐕

Draw cards, make plays and resolve any Chases, then discard down to the
current hand limits. Take another Dog turn if any Buns are still in
play. Once all Buns are out of play for the day, score at Dusk.

### Draw cards and check play allowances

At the beginning of each Dog turn, everyone draws up to their current hand
limit. A full hand draws nothing. The Dog's hand limit is **5 or the number
of Bun players, whichever is higher**. A Bun player's hand limit is
**3 + 1 for each of their Roaming or escaped Buns**.

The Dog may make **one Chase per Dog turn**, unless a card grants more.
It may play **one Startle per Chase**, after declaring the Chase and its targets.
Limits for other Dog plays remain to be decided.

The earlier regular/Burrow allowances remain recorded below; how they
interact with the new card-type limits is still to be decided. They do not
replace the explicit response limits in the Chase sequence.

| **Bun** status | Earlier play allowance per **Dog** turn |
| --- | --- |
| Roaming | Up to 1 regular play |
| Escaped | Up to 1 Burrow play |
| Injured or eaten | No plays |

Each escaped Bun provides one Burrow play. Florian's agreed Burrow ability
allows its owner, when a Chase starts against their other Bun, to draw one
Survival card optionally. If it is Panic, they may use it in response to
Startle against that chased Bun;
discard it if unused. This ability is not a rule for every Bun.

**Empty deck.** When a deck runs out, shuffle its discard pile to make a new draw pile. Continue any remaining draws.

### Phases and steps

A phase contains steps. Complete them in order; passing a step closes it.
A failed Guard does not reopen earlier response steps for its helpers.
Resolve ties within the tie-resolution step, without returning to Panic.
If an effect cancels the Chase, stop that Chase. A card-granted additional
Chase begins a new Chase sequence at Declare Chase and targets, only after
the previous Chase is completely finished. It follows the same rules,
with its own Startle and response opportunities, before Cleanup. It does
not restart Refresh, Prowl, or Preparation. Declare one target per Chase;
additional Chases replace simultaneous multi-target Chase cards.

| Phase | Steps, in order |
| --- | --- |
| **Refresh** | Draw up to hand limits. |
| **Prowl** | Play and resolve Prowl and Lure cards. |
| **Preparation** | Place Prep and play Scavenge. |
| **Chase** | Declare Chase and targets; play Startle; Panic response to Startle; other players' Nice and Nasty; chased owner's Prep; declare Guards; resolve Guard; Chase rolls; resolve ties; apply outcomes and scoring. |
| **Cleanup** | Discard down to hand limits; end turn. |

Prowl resolves immediately. Lures can affect subsequent Prep and Scavenge
as their card text specifies. Each Bun player may place **one new Prep
face down per Dog turn**. Each Roaming Bun provides **one Scavenge
opportunity per Dog turn**, using that Bun's own Scavenge value plus the
card's modifier on a d20 check. A total of 16 or higher succeeds. A natural
20 always succeeds and a natural 1 always fails, regardless of modifiers.
Burrowed Buns do not contribute. See Card Types for the
Scavenge Check and remaining details.

### Prep cards

Keep Prep face down in its owner's area, without assigning it to a Bun
in advance. Choose its target when activating it, following its text.
Only the chased Bun's owner may activate Prep, and only for that chased
Bun. Other players cannot activate Prep in response to that Chase.

There is no limit to the number of Prep cards kept face down. Activation
is optional; unused Prep may stay face down indefinitely. A player may
decline to activate even an applicable Prep. Discard Prep after activation
and resolution. Placing a new Prep and flipping an existing Prep are
different actions; the one-per-Dog-turn placement limit does not limit
the owner's flips during their own Buns' Chase.

Timing belongs in the named effect's definition. See Effects for Cozy.
If an effect cancels a Chase, stop that Chase without Chase rolls.

### Hidden **Buns**

Buns enter play face down. Only their Brace’s owner may look at them.
Being targeted by a Chase does not reveal a Bun. Reveal it when it is
caught or when a revealing effect, such as a Prowl card, reveals it.

When a Dog card affects a hidden Bun, that Bun’s owner must honestly
report whether the effect applies. Prowl cards reveal Buns and cannot
catch them; catching requires a Chase.

### 🎲 Resolve a **Chase**

1. **Declare Chase and targets.** The Dog plays Chase and selects one
   eligible Bun as its target. The existing limit is one
   Chase against a Bun per Dog turn; Divert and Guard interactions
   with that limit remain open.
2. **Play Startle.** The Dog may play one Startle for this Chase.
3. **Panic response to Startle.** Only the chased Bun's owner may play
   Panic for that Bun, and only in response to Startle. The existing
   allowance is one Panic. Without Startle, there is no Panic response.
4. **Other players' Nice and Nasty.** Other Bun players act clockwise.
   For each chased Bun, each other player may play one Nice and one
   Nasty, or pass. These options are independent and optional.
5. **Chased owner's Prep.** Only the chased Bun's owner may activate
   Prep, and only for that chased Bun. The owner may activate any number
   of applicable Prep. If the Chase is canceled, stop.
6. **Declare Guards.** All players secretly choose Guard or Pass for each
   eligible Bun using its reusable choice card. Place the chosen end toward
   the Dog, face down beside that Bun. Once everyone has committed, choices
   are locked. Reveal together with a sideways flip, keeping the same end
   toward the Dog; do not flip end over end or rotate the card. Follow the
   sideways arrows on the back. Each Guard choice requires a separate Guard
   card from hand; Pass spends none. Choices cannot be taken back.
   Each Guard card
   commits one Roaming Bun you control to protect one chased Bun. Choose
   both Buns for each card. You may play at most one Guard card per Roaming
   Bun you control, using a different guarding Bun for each card. Burrowed,
   injured, and eaten Buns cannot guard. If you played a Nasty card against
   a Bun this Dog turn, you cannot play Guard to protect that Bun for the
   rest of the turn, including additional Chases. The window then closes. Being
   targeted does not prevent a player from using Guard or risking
   another of their Buns.
7. **Resolve Guard.** If Guard was played, resolve the Cunning contest
   below. Success cancels the Chase. Failure adds the participating
   helpers to the Chase; it does not reopen Panic, Prep, or Guard.
8. **Chase rolls.** The Dog makes its catch roll and each chased Bun
   makes its escape roll using d20s. Against a face-down Bun, the Dog
   takes **−6 to its catch roll**. Check Natural Rolls first; otherwise
   compare totals after the Bun's Chase modifier, the hidden-Bun penalty,
   and applicable card modifiers. The Dog's roll procedure against
   helpers exposed by a failed Guard remains to be decided.
9. **Resolve ties.** Both sides reroll a tied Chase until it resolves.
   This does not permit another Panic or reopen earlier response steps.
10. **Apply outcomes and scoring.** Apply capture or escape, the Dog's
    Eat or Injure choice when applicable, and the scoring rules below.

#### Guard and Cunning

Guard is an immediate card held in hand, not a face-down Prep card.
Buns work together: add the participating Buns' **Cunning** values to one
shared d20 roll, rolled by the chased Bun's owner. The Dog rolls a d20
and adds its own Cunning.

- **Buns win:** the Dog is driven off and the Chase is canceled. Guard
  does not kill the Dog.
- **Dog wins:** the Dog Chases all participating Buns, including the
  helpers. Each must make a Chase roll and risks capture. For each Bun
  caught, the Dog chooses its outcome under Eat or Injure below.

Natural 1s and 20s still apply to the Guard contest. Detailed Guard tie,
reroll, and natural-roll scoring rules remain to be settled; do not assume
that Guard opens additional Panic windows.

The design intention is for a Dog's Cunning to be substantially higher
than an individual Bun's, encouraging cooperation. For example, Buns
with Cunning 4 and 3 roll d20 + 7 against a Dog with Cunning 12 rolling
d20 + 12. These values are illustrative, not assigned card stats.

#### Chase roll outcomes

| **Natural Rolls** | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Both roll **1**, or both roll **20** | Both reroll. |
| **Dog** rolls **20**, or **Bun** rolls **1** | The Dog catches the Bun. |
| **Bun** rolls **20**, or **Dog** rolls **1** | The Bun escapes. |
| Neither rolls a 1 or 20 | Higher Dog total means a catch. Higher Bun total means an escape. Equal totals mean both sides reroll. |

**Ties and rerolls.** A tied Chase continues against the same Bun.
Both the Dog and the Bun reroll; neither keeps the previous result.
This also applies when both roll a Natural 1 or both roll a Natural 20.
Repeat until the Chase resolves. Rerolls do not open a Panic opportunity;
Panic is only a response to Startle.

In a regular Chase, a Natural 20 earns **+1 point** and a Natural 1
costs **1 point** for the side that rolled it. Apply both adjustments if
both sides qualify. The Bun adjustment goes to the targeted Bun's
player. Count only the roll that resolves the Chase. Record these points
for Dusk in addition to the usual outcome scores.

**Example:** Against a revealed Bun, the Dog’s catch roll is **14**.
The Bun’s escape roll is **12** with a printed **+3 Chase Modifier**.
The Bun’s total is **15**, so it escapes immediately.

Buns stay in play until chased. Winning a Chase is enough to escape for
the day. There are no Escape cards.

#### 🐕 Eat or Injure

When the Dog catches a Bun, reveal that Bun and choose one.

- **Eat it.** Remove that Bun from the game permanently. The Dog earns **+1**,
  the Bun player loses **1 point**, and the usual Brace bonuses apply.
- **Injure it.** The Bun is injured and sits out for the rest of the
  day. The Dog rolls a d20. A roll of **1–10** gives an injury multiplier
  of **1**. A roll of **11–20** gives a multiplier of **2**. Multiply
  **(previous escapes + 1)** by the injury multiplier
  and subtract that many points from the Bun’s player. Count only escapes
  by that same Bun on earlier days. The Dog scores no points for releasing it.

| Earlier escapes | **Bun** player | **Dog** |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 0 | **−1 or −2** | 0 |
| 1 | **−2 or −4** | 0 |
| 2 | **−3 or −6** | 0 |

An injured Bun cannot be chased again that day and gives its owner no Burrow play.

### Finish the **Dog** turn

**End the turn.** After all plays and Chases for the full turn are finished,
each player discards down to their current maximum hand size. Use each
Bun’s status at the end of the turn to calculate its player’s limit.
