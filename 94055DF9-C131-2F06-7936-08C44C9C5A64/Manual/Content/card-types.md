### Bun

Bun cards are the characters controlled by Bun players. Each player starts with two Buns, called a Brace. They begin face down; their owner may look at them. A Bun can escape, be injured, or be eaten. Escaped Buns provide Burrow plays. The same escaped or injured Bun returns at Dawn; an eaten Bun is replaced. Cunning contributes to Guard, while the Chase modifier affects escape rolls.

### Nice

Nice cards help another player's Bun. During the other-player response window, each other Bun player may play one Nice card for each Bun being chased, or pass. Follow the card's targeting and effect text.

### Nasty

Nasty cards make trouble for another player's Bun. During the other-player response window, each other Bun player may play one Nasty card for each Bun being chased, or pass. This option is independent of Nice.

### Panic

Panic is a reaction to Startle. Only the chased Bun's owner may play it, and only for that chased Bun. The existing allowance is one Panic. Without Startle, there is no Panic opportunity; Guard failure and Chase rerolls do not reopen it.

### Prep

Each Bun player may place one Prep face down per Dog turn during the preparation window. Keep it in its owner's area without assigning a Bun in advance; there is no limit to the number kept face down. Activation is optional, and the card's named effect defines its timing. Choose the target when activating it and discard it after resolution. During a Chase, only the chased Bun's owner may flip Prep, and only for that chased Bun. The owner may flip any number of applicable Prep. Other players cannot activate Prep in response to that Chase. See Effects and Take a Dog Turn for the full response sequence.

### Scavenge

Scavenge is a Survival card type played during Preparation. Each Roaming
Bun provides one Scavenge opportunity per Dog turn and uses its own
Scavenge value for its check. Two Roaming Buns provide two independent
opportunities; one provides one; none provides none. Burrowed, injured,
and eaten Buns do not contribute opportunities or values. Do not average
Buns' values or transfer one Bun's opportunity to another.

A Scavenge card requires a successful **Scavenge Check** before its effect
can be carried out. Roll a d20 and add the acting Bun's Scavenge value
and the Scavenge card's modifier. A modified total of **16 or higher**
succeeds. A **natural 20 always succeeds** and a **natural 1 always fails**,
regardless of modifiers.

Only Bun cards have a Scavenge value. Initial values are randomly assigned
integers from **1 to 10**. Scavenge cards specify their own positive or
negative check modifiers; powerful effects can carry negative modifiers.

Scavenge concerns finding and exchanging supplies, not Chase-roll bonuses.
Effects may involve your hand, the Survival deck, the shared Survival
discard pile, or your own face-down Prep, as the card specifies. Discarded
Prep can come from any player. Other players' hands remain off-limits.
Specific card effects, card count, and what happens to a Scavenge card
after a failed check remain to be decided; the cards await implementation.

### Guard

Guard is played directly from hand during the Guard step, after the chased
owner's Prep and before Chase rolls. The chased player has the first
opportunity, followed clockwise by each other Bun player once.

Each Guard card commits **one Roaming Bun you control** to protect
**one chased Bun**. Choose the guarding Bun and the Bun it will protect.
During your opportunity, you may play up to one Guard card per Roaming
Bun you control, using a different guarding Bun for each card. You must
have a Guard card for each Bun you commit. Burrowed, injured, and eaten
Buns cannot be committed as guards. The helper count is not rolled and
requires no printed Guard value.

**Nasty prevents Guard:** If you played a Nasty card against a Bun during
this Dog turn, you cannot play a Guard card to protect that Bun for the
rest of the turn. This restriction applies to that player and that Bun,
including any additional Chases during the same turn.

The chased Bun's owner rolls the shared defense d20 and adds the
participating Buns' Cunning values, against the Dog's d20 plus Cunning. Success cancels the Chase without killing the
Dog. Failure exposes all participating Buns, including helpers, to the
Chase without reopening earlier response steps. Detailed ties, combining
defended groups, and eligibility of already-chased helpers remain open;
see Take a Dog Turn and Still to Decide.

### Dog

The Dog character is controlled by the Dog player. Draw one Dog at setup and place it face up. Its traits can be referenced by card effects, and its Cunning is used in Guard contests. The Dog uses Havoc cards to pursue Buns. When it catches a Bun, it chooses whether to eat or injure it under the Chase rules.

### Chase

A Chase card starts a pursuit against one eligible Bun and specifies any card-specific effects. The default limit is one Chase per Dog turn; a card may grant more. After declaring Chase and targets, resolve Startle, its Panic response, other players' Nice and Nasty, the chased owner's Prep, and Guard before Chase rolls. A Chase canceled by an effect stops without Chase rolls. The existing limit is one Chase against a Bun per Dog turn, with Redirect and Guard interactions still open.

Some cards allow the Dog to make a second or additional Chase during the
same turn. **Finish the current Chase completely before starting the next.**
Treat each additional Chase as a brand-new Chase, following the same rules
and full sequence: choose an eligible target, optionally play Startle,
resolve its Panic response, Nice and Nasty, the chased owner's Prep,
Guard, Chase rolls, ties, outcomes, and scoring. Each Chase has its own
Startle and response opportunities. Additional Chases do not restart
Refresh, Prowl, or Preparation, and do not bypass the existing limit of
one Chase against a Bun per Dog turn. Proceed to Cleanup after all Chases
are finished. Additional Chases replace simultaneous multi-target Chase
cards; helpers exposed by a failed Guard are still handled under Guard.

### Lure

Lure is a Havoc card played in the Dog's Prowl-and-Lure window before Bun preparation. It can bait Buns or affect subsequent Prep and Scavenge plays as its text specifies. Its play limit and individual effects remain to be defined.

### Prowl

Prowl is a Havoc card that reveals Buns without catching them. The Dog may play Prowl in the Prowl-and-Lure window, and it resolves immediately. Catching still requires a Chase. Cozy checks whether the Dog has used Prowl on that particular Bun; its Prowl lookback period remains undecided.

### Startle

The Dog may play one Startle after declaring the Chase and its targets. Startle opens a Panic response only for the chased Bun's owner, for that Bun. Startle is a Havoc card; individual effects remain to be defined.

## Dog traits

Buns and Dogs have **traits**, which card effects can refer to. Check the
traits printed on the character card when an effect names a particular trait.
Trait names have no intrinsic mechanical effect; the card text defines what
happens when it refers to a trait.

The current Dog traits are:

- **Cruel**
- **Cunning**
- **Merciless**
- **Patient**
- **Ravenous**
- **Reckless**
- **Relentless**

A Dog can have more than one trait. For example, Gallows is **Ravenous**
and **Relentless**. An effect that applies only against **Reckless** Dogs
does not apply to Gallows based on those printed traits.

## Cunning value

Cunning is the agreed name for a comparative numeric card stat. In Guard,
the participating Buns combine their values against the Dog's value.
Individual values and other uses remain to be designed. The existing
Dog trait also named Cunning is distinct from this new numeric stat;
its naming needs review before implementation.
