# 🃏 The Cards

## 🃏 Cards Types
| Side | Set | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🐇 **Bun** | **Bun** | Your Bun characters. |
| 🐇 **Bun** | **Nice** | Helps another player’s Bun. |
| 🐇 **Bun** | **Nasty** | Makes trouble for another player’s Bun. |
| 🐇 **Bun** | **Panic** | A reaction during a Chase, after Prep and before Guard; another may be played before each tied Chase reroll. |
| 🐇 **Bun** | **Prep** | Place one face down per Dog turn during the Bun preparation window. Flip optionally at its effect's timing, choose eligible targets, then discard after resolution. See the Chase sequence for flip limits. |
| 🐇 **Bun** | **Scavenge** | Searches decks, recovers discards, or inspects hands as specified by the card. Played during the Bun preparation window. |
| 🐇 **Bun** | **Guard** | Played from hand after Panic to commit Buns to a shared Cunning defense before Chase rolls. Failure exposes the helpers to the Chase. |
| 🐕 **Dog** | **Dog** | The Dog characters. |
| 🐕 **Dog** | **Chase** | Starts a Chase and can specify how it’s affected. |
| 🐕 **Dog** | **Lure** | Played with Sniff before Bun preparation; may affect Prep and Scavenge as specified by the card. |
| 🐕 **Dog** | **Sniff** | Reveals Buns without catching them. |
| 🐕 **Dog** | **Startle** | Up to one before declaring each Chase; individual effects remain to be defined. |

## Dog traits

Buns and Dogs have **traits**, which card effects can refer to. Check the
traits printed on the character card when an effect names a particular trait.

The current Dog traits are:

- **Cruel**
- **Cunning**
- **Merciless**
- **Patient**
- **Ravenous**
- **Reckless**
- **Relentless**

A Dog can have more than one trait. For example, Gallows is **Ravenous**
and **Relentless**. An effect that applies only against **Reckless** Dogs
does not apply to Gallows based on those printed traits.

## Four decks
###### Shuffled Before First Draw and Kept Face Down.

| Deck | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| **Bun** | Bun characters only;  |
| **Dog** | Dog characters only; shuffled and kept face down. |
| **Havoc** | The Dog’s action deck: Chase, Lure, Sniff, and Startle. |
| **Survival** | The Buns’ shared action deck: Nice, Nasty, Panic, Prep, Scavenge, and Guard. Scavenge and Guard are agreed types awaiting card implementation. |

## Cunning value

Cunning is the agreed name for a comparative numeric card stat. In Guard,
the participating Buns combine their values against the Dog's value.
Individual values and other uses remain to be designed. The existing
Dog trait also named Cunning is distinct from this new numeric stat;
its naming needs review before implementation.
