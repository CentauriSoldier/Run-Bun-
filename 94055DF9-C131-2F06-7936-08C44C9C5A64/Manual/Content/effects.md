## Effects

Effects is the shared term for named abilities and statuses. Their timing
and rules are defined here so cards can give short targeting instructions.

### Cozy

When a Chase is declared, activate Cozy during the applicable Prep
response window, before Panic and Guard. The card chooses the target;
Safe and Sound may choose any Roaming Bun, including another player's.

A Cozy Bun cannot be targeted by a Chase unless the Dog has sniffed out
**that Bun**. If Cozy is applied in response to a Chase that it prevents,
cancel that Chase. Sniffing a different Bun does not bypass Cozy.

**Safe and Sound card wording:** Choose any Roaming Bun. Apply Cozy.

Cozy's duration and whether an earlier turn's Sniff counts remain
undecided. The result when Cozy protects only some targets of a
multi-Bun Chase also remains undecided.

### Other agreed card effects

**Just Wide Enough:** +3 to escape roll versus Reckless Dogs.

**Florian — Burrow:** When a Chase starts against your other Bun, you may
draw 1 Survival card. If it is Panic, you may play it in that Chase.
Discard it if you do not play it.
